The opportunity

About the position

An established engineering and construction contractor in Doha is seeking qualified Electrical Technicians for commercial and industrial projects. Candidates must hold relevant technical qualifications and experience.

Sample employer: International Engineering Contractor – Qatar

Key responsibilities

  • Install, maintain, and repair electrical systems and equipment
  • Read and interpret electrical drawings and specifications
  • Test electrical systems and identify faults
  • Ensure all work meets Qatar electrical standards
  • Maintain records of maintenance and repairs

What the role requires

Candidate requirements

  • NVQ Level 3 or equivalent in Electrical Engineering
  • Minimum 3 years of practical electrical experience
  • Knowledge of industrial electrical systems
  • Ability to read technical drawings

Qualifications

  • NVQ Level 3 / Technical College Certificate in Electrical Engineering
