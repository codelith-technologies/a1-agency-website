Recruitment Journey

How the Recruitment Process Works

A general guide to overseas recruitment. Exact steps and their order vary by vacancy, employer, destination and applicable official requirements.

  1. 01

    Explore Opportunities

    Explore roles by country or field. Review the requirements carefully; current website vacancies are sample listings.

  2. 02

    Submit Application

    Contact A-One about current application arrangements. The website’s demo form does not send or store applications or CVs.

  3. 03

    Initial Screening

    Candidate information may be reviewed against a confirmed vacancy’s eligibility, experience and document requirements.

  4. 04

    Employer Selection / Interview

    Interviews or assessments may apply. If selected, review the offer, contract and confirmed employment terms before proceeding.

  5. 05

    Documents & Official Requirements

    Confirm applicable documents, medical checks, registrations or clearances. Requirements vary by country and vacancy.

  6. 06

    Visa / Travel Preparation

    Complete applicable visa, entry-permit and travel preparation steps. Confirm the required approvals; visa approval is not guaranteed.

  7. 07

    Pre-Departure Guidance

    Verify travel details, employment documents, employer contacts and any required orientation or official instructions.

  8. 08

    Departure & Overseas Employment

    Travel once approvals and arrangements are confirmed. Verify arrival instructions and agreed employer contacts before departure.
