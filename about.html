About A-One

Connecting Sri Lankan talent with global opportunities.

A-One Foreign Employment Agency connects Sri Lankan job seekers with overseas career opportunities while supporting international employers seeking Sri Lankan manpower.

People. Possibilities. Connections.

Who we are

A recruitment bridge,
built around people.

Local understanding. International opportunity.

A-One Foreign Employment Agency is a Sri Lanka-focused recruitment agency connecting the aspirations of candidates with the workforce needs of international employers.

Our approach brings together clear communication, candidate guidance and recruitment coordination, with attention to the people on both sides of every opportunity.

  • Candidate and employer services
  • Overseas careers and international manpower enquiries

Our purpose

One shared direction.
Meaningful connections.

01

Our Vision

To become a trusted bridge between Sri Lankan talent and reputable international employers, creating sustainable global career opportunities.

02

Our Mission

To connect Sri Lankan workers with responsible overseas employers through transparent, professional and people-focused recruitment services.

Our recruitment approach

People first.
Clarity at every step.

The principles that shape how we support candidates, understand employers and coordinate the recruitment journey.

  • 01

    Clear communication

    Clear job information and straightforward conversations help candidates and employers understand the next step.

  • 02

    Candidate guidance

    Support with application steps, documentation and pre-departure preparation keeps people at the centre of the process.

  • 03

    Employer understanding

    Recruitment starts with understanding the role, the working environment and the employer’s manpower requirements.

  • 04

    Coordinated recruitment

    From screening and interviews to documentation, each stage forms part of a considered recruitment journey.
