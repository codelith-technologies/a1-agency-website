The employer journey

From requirement
to recruitment.

A clear sequence with your business involved at the points that matter. Exact steps and timing depend on the role, destination and confirmed engagement.

Request Manpower
  1. 01

    Share your workforce requirements

    Tell us about your company, job roles, required experience and proposed employment terms so the team can review your request.

  2. 02

    Candidate sourcing

    The recruitment team sources candidates in relation to the requirements discussed with your business.

  3. 03

    Screening & shortlisting

    Candidate experience, qualifications and relevant documents are reviewed to prepare profiles for employer consideration.

  4. 04

    Employer interviews & selection

    Your team interviews shortlisted candidates online or in person and makes the final selection for each role.

  5. 05

    Documentation coordination

    Selected candidates are guided through role-specific documents and checks. The parties coordinate applicable destination and Sri Lankan requirements through official channels.

  6. 06

    Pre-departure & deployment support

    Candidates complete the confirmed preparation steps before travel, with departure arrangements coordinated with the employer.
